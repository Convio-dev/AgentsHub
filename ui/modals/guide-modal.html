Antigravity Profiles & Google Sign-In

AgentsHub manages isolated multi-account profiles for Antigravity without touching your main IDE login. Everything is stored 100% locally on your machine — all tokens, credentials, and settings never leave your PC.

1
Add Profile: Go to Antigravity Profiles or click + Add Profile on the Dashboard. Name it (e.g. Account_2).
2
Launch Session: Select your profile and open an Antigravity Terminal. The CLI prompts for Google authentication.
3
Sign in with Google: Authorize via browser. AgentsHub automatically intercepts google_accounts.json and extracts your email into profile metadata.
4
Seamless Multi-Account: Switch active profiles anytime from the Dashboard, Terminals tab, or mobile bot.
100% Stored Locally: All Google OAuth tokens and credentials stay isolated in your local %LOCALAPPDATA%\AgentsHub\profiles directory. Zero cloud syncing, zero external telemetry.

Antigravity Multi-Account Profiles & Google Sign-In

Antigravity CLI stores Google credentials in %USERPROFILE%\.gemini. AgentsHub virtualizes this storage so you can maintain unlimited distinct Google profiles without logging out of your main Google account or Antigravity IDE. All profile credentials, tokens, and data are stored 100% locally on your machine.

Step 1

Create Profile

Navigate to Antigravity Profiles (or click + Add Profile on Dashboard). Provide a profile name like Account_2 or WorkProfile.

Step 2

Select & Launch Terminal

Select the new profile from the dropdown and open a terminal session (Standalone or embedded). AgentsHub sets the virtualized Gemini home directory for that process.

Step 3

Authenticate with Google Account

The Antigravity CLI will prompt for authentication: it generates a login URL. Open the link in your browser and authorize with your target Google account.

Step 4

Automatic Credential Sync & Verification

AntigravityProfileService watches google_accounts.json and oauth_creds.json, parses the email, and persists it to the profile. A verified email badge will appear next to the profile name.

100% Local Storage & Privacy: Everything is stored locally on your device under %LOCALAPPDATA%\AgentsHub\profiles. No external databases, no cloud servers, and no token telemetry. Your Google credentials never leave your machine.
Switching Profiles on the Fly
Use the profile dropdown in the top header, the Dashboard card, or mobile Pocket Commander: /active [profile] or /run [profile].

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add another Google account for Antigravity?
Open the Antigravity Profiles tab, click + Add Profile, give it a name, launch a terminal session with that profile, and complete Google OAuth in the browser. The credentials isolate automatically.
Why use the Standalone Terminals window over the embedded tab?
The Standalone Window is an unconstrained native window built specifically for multi-monitor setups, ConPTY pseudoconsole inputs, and split sessions.
How do I switch all bots offline?
Go to Settings → Remote Webhooks → Pocket Commander and flip the Master Remote Bot Switch to OFF.
Where are workspace safety snapshots stored?
In .agents/snapshots/ inside your project workspace folder. You can inspect or revert them at any time.
